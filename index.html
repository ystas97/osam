Most engagements run about 4 weeks, broken into clear gates. You see work weekly, not monthly.

1 week Research

Research

We'll discuss your goals, audience, business needs, and project scope. Then we'll define the direction, priorities, and next steps.

2 week Concept

Concept

We'll explore visual or product directions: mood, structure, style, references, and key ideas. You'll choose the direction that fits your goals best.

3 week Design

Design

We'll create the main design deliverables — whether it's a logo, website, landing page, app screens, or brand materials — and prepare them for review.

4 week Handoff

Handoff

We hand off files, guidelines, and support so the work lands cleanly.
