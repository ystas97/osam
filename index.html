About Team

I'm Viktor, founder of OSAM design studio

I founded OSAM and I'm the designer behind every product we make.

For 6 years I've worked as a senior designer on mobile apps and web platforms. I've helped teams grow real product design expertise, build processes that don't collapse at the first deadline, and turn into strong teams that ship things that actually work.

And over and over I watched great designs die in development. Gorgeous screens in Figma, and at launch something nobody recognized. Like a dream house that turns out to be a tent

So I started OSAM, where design and development live under one roof. No lost handoffs, no "close enough", no tents. What you approve is what your users get ✨
