Corpsoft24 is a digital ecosystem that gives businesses access to corporate IT services through a single-window experience.

OSAM designed a responsive B2B website that organises a broad service portfolio, company expertise, customer stories and professional content into one coherent interface.

The challenge: making a complex ecosystem easy to navigate

Corporate technology services often involve several audiences, technical categories and different levels of detail. Corpsoft24 needed to explain cloud solutions, business software and project services without overwhelming decision-makers or hiding useful information behind industry language.

We created a clear information architecture that moves from a high-level value proposition into individual services, proof points and case studies. Consistent navigation and content patterns help visitors compare options and understand where each solution fits.

A responsive B2B website built around clarity

The UX/UI system uses modular service cards, structured benefits and focused calls to action. This creates a predictable journey across service pages while leaving enough flexibility for specialised products and technical explanations.

Mobile layouts preserve the same hierarchy in a more compact format. Key information remains readable, navigation stays accessible and customer stories continue to support trust throughout the experience.

Complex corporate services organised into one clear digital system.

Content, expertise and customer proof in one system

The website connects the commercial service catalogue with a community and editorial layer. Articles, webinars, news and customer stories give the platform depth while reusable filters and cards make a growing library easier to explore.

The result is a scalable design system that can support new services and content without losing consistency. Explore OSAM’s approach to B2B website design or contact us about a complex digital platform.
