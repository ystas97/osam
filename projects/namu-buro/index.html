Namu Buro creates interior spaces and produces its own designer furniture. The studio needed a digital presence that could present both disciplines as one clear architectural practice.

OSAM developed the website, identity, printed materials and social media design as a connected visual system focused on projects, materials and the character of the studio.

The challenge: letting the work lead

An architecture studio website has to communicate expertise without competing with the portfolio. Namu Buro works across interiors, objects and furniture, so the structure needed to make this range understandable while preserving a calm, editorial atmosphere.

We created a clear content hierarchy for project stories, services and studio information. Generous spacing, controlled typography and image-led layouts give each interior room to breathe and help potential clients understand the practice quickly.

A flexible architecture portfolio website

The UX/UI system supports different project scales and image formats without making the website feel inconsistent. Reusable layouts allow the team to publish new residential, commercial and furniture work while maintaining the same visual rhythm.

Navigation stays intentionally simple. Visitors can move from a strong first impression to detailed project imagery, understand the studio’s capabilities and reach out without unnecessary interaction or visual noise.

A restrained identity built around space, material and detail.

One identity across digital and print

The visual identity was designed to work beyond the website. Typography, composition and graphic details extend naturally to printed materials and social media, giving Namu Buro a recognisable voice wherever its work appears.

The result is a practical design system that supports ongoing communication and keeps the studio’s architecture at the centre. Explore OSAM’s website design process or contact us about a new digital project.
