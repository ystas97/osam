Petr Safioulline is an architect, founder of Yaratam bureau and creator of his own furniture line.

OSAM designed a portfolio website and visual identity that bring architectural projects, professional services and furniture work into one coherent presentation.

The challenge: one personal practice, several disciplines

The website needed to communicate an individual architectural point of view while covering a broad body of work. Architecture, interiors, furniture and professional services each require different information, but they still need to feel like parts of the same practice.

We built a clear hierarchy around visual project stories and concise service information. The experience allows potential clients to discover the work first, then understand capabilities and move naturally toward consultation.

A portfolio system for architecture and furniture

The UX/UI uses modular project layouts that can support different scales, image ratios and amounts of supporting text. This flexibility keeps the portfolio consistent as new architecture and furniture projects are added.

Service information and consultation paths remain visible without interrupting the editorial presentation. On mobile, the same hierarchy preserves the impact of the imagery while keeping navigation and project details easy to use.

Identity that supports the work instead of decorating it

The visual language is deliberately restrained. Typography, layout and graphic details create recognition while allowing drawings, spaces, materials and furniture to remain the main voice of the practice.

The system extends to social communication and creates a consistent foundation for future projects. Explore OSAM’s approach to architecture website design or contact us about a new portfolio.
