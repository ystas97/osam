[01/10] 2number

← Corpsoft24 TCG Card Scanner →

2number lets people buy a phone number right on their iPhone and receive calls and messages on it. One for work, one for side projects, one for that marketplace listing you’d rather not have calling your personal number at 11 pm.

OSAM took the product from idea to App Store: UX/UI design and native iOS development, all under one roof.

  • UX/UI design
  • Mobile app design
  • iOS development

iOS app design and development for buying phone numbers

Telecom apps often feel like a contract nobody wants to read: tiny print, confusing plans, buttons hiding in plain sight. We went the other way 📞

One clear flow: pick a number, start using it

Getting a number should take a moment, not a manual. We built the journey around one simple path, from choosing a number to receiving the first call or message, with nothing in between that needs decoding.

Calls and messages in one calm place

Incoming calls and messages live in a clean, familiar interface, so a second number feels as natural to use as the first one.
